Where you live decides who you encounter.

Every colour on this map is a Houston neighbourhood, painted by the crowds its residents actually mix with when they shop, eat, and go about their month. Blue neighbourhoods travel into much richer crowds than themselves. Orange ones travel into crowds of a different race. Dark slate stays among people like itself. Cream is unlike on both.

The pattern is sharp, it is geographic, and it barely moves in six years. Press play and watch.
